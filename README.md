### 👨‍💻 About Me

I am a **Mechanical Engineering** student in the **Talent Engineer Program** at **Ho Chi Minh City University of Technology (HCMUT - VNU-HCM)**.

My work lies at the intersection of **Computational Mechanics**, **Physics-Informed Machine Learning (PINNs)**, and **Computer Vision & Robotics**.

- 🔭 **Current Research & Focus:**
  - Physics-Informed Neural Networks (PINNs) for structural & composite plate analysis.
  - Computer Vision pipelines (3D camera calibration, stereo vision, object tracking with YOLO & OpenCV).
  - Numerical simulation & continuum mechanics (FEA / CFD / Plate & Shell theories).
- 🛠️ **Engineering Toolkit:** Python, C/C++, PyTorch, OpenCV, MATLAB, LaTeX, Ansys, SolidWorks.
- 🌐 **Languages:** Vietnamese (Native), English (TOEIC 965), Japanese (JLPT N3 / N2 candidate).
